Reference comparison

Read-only inspection of the supplied Klass demo confirmed role-specific navigation, course records with monthly/day/installment payment types, and payment review states. The admin navigation also exposes exams, attendance, tute dispatch, media, reports, staff, integrations and smart cards. The teacher account exposes Home, Courses, Exams and Media Library. The student account exposes course discovery and course-level Content, Exams, Live Classes, Tute Delivery and About tabs. The inspected course had no sessions. All three supplied role accounts were inspected read-only and signed out; no reference data was changed.

Klass pricing was used as a comparison, not a claim of identical capability. SISU retains the requested per-active-class enrolment billing rule. A full one-for-one feature parity claim is not supported by this review.
