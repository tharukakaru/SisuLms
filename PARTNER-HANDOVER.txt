CONFIRMED BUSINESS RULES
Selected plan trial: 14 days. Base monthly LKR: Individual 2500, Silver 5000, Gold 15000, Platinum 22000.
Usage LKR per active student: 100/50/50/50. Distinct student present at least once per provider billing period; begin accrual after trial ends and verified payment coverage begins.
Estimate five days before period end; final invoice at period end; due ten days later. Provider pays usage; no direct student usage charge.
These are requirements, not a guarantee every legacy code path already implements them. See CONFIRMED-BILLING-DECISIONS.txt.
