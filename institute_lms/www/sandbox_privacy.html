SISU Academy — Sandbox Privacy Notice

Version: sandbox-2026-10-31-v1. This notice covers authorized SISU LMS sandbox testing operated by Teen Academy Pvt Ltd. It is not the notice for a publicly launched service. Use your own test details; do not upload other people's information or real student records.

Information used during testing

Registration collects your name, email, mobile number, username, country, time zone, chosen role and role-specific details such as study level, teaching subject or institute information. Optional profile information is labelled optional. We process password hashes, verification state, security logs and account permissions. Verification emails and their codes may remain in the email queue and provider logs. Please provide a recovery contact only with that person's permission.

When you use enabled LMS features, the sandbox may also hold class membership, assignments, progress, uploaded materials, messages, subscription records and payment-test references. Browser notification testing stores a device token after you opt in. Do not enter real card details into sandbox payment tests.

Why we use this information

We use it to verify contact ownership, create and secure test accounts, route users to their role, test teaching and learning functions, enforce access and subscription rules, investigate errors and deliver requested transactional messages. Email and phone verification does not constitute marketing consent or a provider endorsement.

Services and people with access

Authorized administrators and the teachers or institute staff responsible for the features you test may access relevant records according to their permissions. Brevo processes verification emails; Notify.lk and mobile networks process verification SMS. Google Firebase processes enabled browser push notifications. Google sign-in, Cloudflare, LiveKit, Meta WhatsApp and CyberSource may process relevant data when their respective features are enabled and used. Some integrations remain disabled or under test. These providers may process data outside Sri Lanka.

Storage and security

Sandbox records are stored on the test infrastructure and may be included in restricted backups. Access controls, password hashing and protected server-side credentials help safeguard them. No system can guarantee absolute security. Test information is kept while needed for testing, security investigation and applicable recordkeeping; backup copies may remain until those backups are removed. A fixed production retention schedule has not yet been established.

Your choices and requests

You may leave optional fields empty, stop testing, revoke browser notification permission and ask us to review, correct or delete your test information. Some records may need to be retained for security or applicable recordkeeping. Contact info@sisuacademylk.com for privacy questions or requests; if email is unavailable, contact your SISU test coordinator. Never send passwords, API keys or verification codes with a request.

Children and sensitive information

This sandbox is for authorized testers. Do not register children or submit children's or sensitive personal records during this test. Arrangements for a public service serving minors must be established before launch.

Updates

Changes will be identified by a new notice version. Registration records the version presented when you register. The operator must review this notice and establish the production privacy, retention and contact procedures before public launch.
