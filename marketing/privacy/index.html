Your privacy at SISU

Effective 2 November 2026 · Version public-2026-11-02-v1

SISU Academy is a learning platform for students, teachers and institutes, powered by Icorenic Pvt Ltd. This notice explains the information involved when you use our website and learning space. For privacy questions, contact info@sisuacademylk.com.

Some features may be unavailable while setup is completed. This notice does not mean that every described feature is enabled for your account.

Information you provide

  • Account and profile details, such as your name, email, mobile number, username, country, time zone, account type and study or teaching information.
  • Verification information used to check your email and phone, and your password or Google sign-in identity.
  • Optional profile photos, biographies, qualifications, website links and other details you choose to add.
  • When you use learning features: institute memberships, follows, class enrolments, submissions, assessments, attendance, messages and uploaded learning material.

Information used to run the service

The service uses session cookies and browser storage to keep you signed in and remember interface preferences. Server and application records may include IP addresses, request times, errors, verification attempts and security events. These help operate the platform, investigate faults and limit misuse.

How information is used

We use information to create and verify accounts, apply role and classroom permissions, provide learning features, deliver requested verification codes and service updates, respond to enquiries and maintain the security of the service. Optional calendar or notification connections use the settings and permissions you select.

Profiles, institutes and other users

Information you publish in a public profile can be visible to other people. Do not put passwords, verification codes, identity documents or other confidential information in your public biography or posts.

Joining an institute, requesting teacher affiliation or enrolling in a class can make the relevant identity, membership and learning information available to the responsible institute administrators or teachers. A follow does not by itself grant access to private classroom content. Teachers and institutes may also have their own privacy notices for information they collect outside SISU.

Service providers and connections

  • Google: when you choose Google sign-in, SISU receives the account information needed to identify and authenticate you, including your email and basic profile information. SISU does not receive your Google password.
  • Brevo: processes recipient addresses and message content when SISU sends email.
  • Notify.lk: processes mobile numbers and message content when SISU sends SMS verification or service messages.
  • LiveKit: supports live audio/video when a live-class feature is enabled and used. Camera, microphone and screen sharing involve the media you choose to share with a session.
  • Hosting and infrastructure providers: support storage, delivery and operation of the website and application.

Provider processing may occur outside your country. Their own privacy terms also apply to their services. Connecting an optional service may involve additional permissions shown during that connection.

Payment-gateway transactions and WhatsApp delivery are disabled for this launch. Do not send card details through profiles, chat or support enquiries.

Keeping and removing information

Account, learning and operational records may remain stored while they support the service, account administration, security, dispute handling or applicable record-keeping needs. There is no single automatic deletion period for all records. Removing visible content does not necessarily remove associated logs or backup copies immediately.

You can ask about the information held about you, request corrections or request account/content deletion by emailing the contact address below. We may need to verify your identity and clarify the request. Some records may need to be retained or may be controlled by your institute; we will explain relevant limits when responding.

Your choices and younger learners

You can edit available profile fields, manage supported notification settings, stop following providers and disconnect optional integrations where those controls are available. Required verification and account-security messages are separate from optional updates.

Younger learners should involve a parent or guardian when creating an account and sharing information. Parents or guardians can contact us about a child's account. Avoid sharing a child's sensitive information publicly.

Security and changes

SISU uses HTTPS, account verification and access controls. No internet service can guarantee complete security. Keep your login details and verification codes private, and report suspected account misuse promptly.

We may update this notice as the platform changes. The effective date and version above identify the notice currently published. Account setup records the policy version presented during registration.
